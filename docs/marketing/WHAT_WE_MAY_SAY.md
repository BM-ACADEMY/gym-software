# What we may say (cheat sheet)

Pulled from `docs/MARKETING_CLAIMS.md` (gym, last checked 29 Oct 2026) and `docs/products/<x>/MARKETING_CLAIMS.md` (12 Nov 2026). If a claims file changes, those files win over this sheet. Written 13 Nov 2026, not yet tested.

## The six pilot products (Groom, Mart, Dine, Cloth, Wash, Hire)
Every feature is written but not tested by a person. So public copy is only:
- "<Product> is coming. Join the invite-only pilot."
- The one pilot line for that product (it is printed on each page in `marketing/site/`).
- "Pilot is for a small number of invited businesses. Prices are shown here only after we confirm them."
- Language: say "English, Tamil and Hindi are being checked by native speakers". Do not say "in Tamil" as a finished fact yet.
- Data: say "We will explain what we store before you sign up". Do not say export or delete is built.

## GymDesk (true now, per `docs/MARKETING_CLAIMS.md`)
- 14-day free trial, no card. Plans are prepaid, no auto-renewal.
- GST invoice for every payment.
- Import members from Excel or CSV. Import fingerprint or face machine logs from a file.
- WhatsApp reminders for renewals, dues, absence and an owner summary (not on the smallest plan).
- Menus in English, Tamil and Hindi (some screens still English).
- AI workout and diet plans (a trainer or owner reviews before sharing; not medical advice). Do not print the monthly limits.
- AI weekly summary with 3 things to act on. "Ask GymDesk" answers how-to questions from help articles.
- "Member names and phone numbers are never sent to the AI for the weekly summary or the assistant."

## Never say (any product)
- "Guaranteed" anything. "Saves you Rs X". "Brings back N customers". "Increases sales".
- "No. 1", "best", "most trusted", customer counts, ratings, testimonials.
- "Unlimited AI". "AI predicts". "AI replies to your customers". "AI calls".
- "Runs on its own", "fully automatic". We suggest; the owner decides; sending needs approved templates and the owner's switch.
- "We file your GST", "GST-compliant for every case".
- "100% secure", "bank-grade", "never goes down".
- Any price, any competitor name or comparison.

## Product-specific never-say (short)
- Groom: no medical advice, "patient records" or health claims. Not built: reminders, loyalty, stock, attendance.
- Mart: no "works offline", scale or printer support, "reads your supplier bills with AI".
- Dine: no "GST-ready", "FSSAI-compliant", Zomato/Swiggy, QR menu. Nothing that promotes alcohol.
- Cloth: no "correct GST for every garment", barcode billing, fabric by metre.
- Wash: no "customer gets a message when the car is ready", photo proof, "fills your bays".
- Hire: see `docs/products/hire/MARKETING_CLAIMS.md`; candidate messages stay suggest-only until the lawyer's written approval is filed.
- Gym: no live biometric sync, door control, app-store apps, class booking, payroll, multi-branch, member AutoPay, "Renewal Copilot" and "Ask about your own numbers" until a human has tested them.
