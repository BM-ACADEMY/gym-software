# One-page pitches, in the owner's worry words

Written 13 Nov 2026, not yet tested. **All rupee examples are Assumptions** (round numbers to show the idea; not measured, not a promise). Tamil is in `SCRIPTS.md` and needs native review. Use the **Say today** line; the **Worry** is what you ask about, not a claim.

How to use: ask the worry as a question. Listen. Then say the one allowed line and invite to the list. Do not describe features for the six pilot products (not yet seen working).

---
## GymDesk (Fit)
- **Worry (their words):** "Members join in January and vanish by March. I only find out when the fee is not paid." / "Who has dues? I check a notebook." / "My staff takes cash and I cannot check."
- **Rupee example (Assumption):** 10 members who do not renew in a month, at an assumed Rs 1,000 a month each, is Rs 10,000 a month gone. Ask: "How many left last month?"
- **Say today (true now):** "14-day free trial, no card. Prepaid, no auto-renewal. GST invoice for every payment. You can bring your member list from Excel. WhatsApp reminders for renewals and dues."
- **Do not say:** that it stops members leaving, any member counts, any price, live machine sync.
- **Ask:** "Can I put you on the invite list for the first group of gyms?"

## Groom (Salon, Spa, Barber, Clinic)
- **Worry:** "Customers book on phone and I forget." / "My staff gives discount and I never know." / "I do not know who has stopped coming."
- **Rupee example (Assumption):** 8 no-show or forgotten bookings a month at an assumed Rs 400 each is Rs 3,200 a month.
- **Say today (pilot):** "We are inviting a few salons, spas, barbers and clinics to try a simple booking and billing app first."
- **Do not say:** reminders, double-booking protection, staff discount rules, anything for clinics about records or health.
- **Ask:** "Shall I add you to the pilot list?"

## Mini Mart (kirana)
- **Worry:** "Udhaar is not coming back." / "I do not know what is expiring." / "I order stock by guess."
- **Rupee example (Assumption):** Rs 15,000 of udhaar outstanding for 3 months, one in ten never paid, is Rs 1,500 lost.
- **Say today (pilot):** "Billing, stock and udhaar for kirana and mini marts, in simple words. Invite-only pilot first."
- **Do not say:** offline, scale or printer, bill photo reading, reorder or expiry lists as working.
- **Ask:** "May I add your shop to the pilot list?"

## Dine (restaurant, cafe, pub)
- **Worry:** "The kitchen and the counter do not match." / "I do not know my real profit per dish." / "Staff give free items."
- **Rupee example (Assumption):** 5 free or unbilled dishes a day at an assumed Rs 120 each is Rs 600 a day, Rs 18,000 a month.
- **Say today (pilot):** "Orders, kitchen tickets, bills and stock for restaurants and cafes. Invite-only pilot first."
- **Do not say:** GST-ready, FSSAI, Zomato or Swiggy, QR menu, anything about liquor.
- **Ask:** "Would you like to be on the pilot list?"

## Cloth (textile and garment shops)
- **Worry:** "I have stock but not in the size the customer wants." / "Old stock is stuck." / "Alteration orders get lost."
- **Rupee example (Assumption):** Rs 2,00,000 of stock unsold for 6 months is Rs 2,00,000 of cash tied up.
- **Say today (pilot):** "Stock by size and colour, billing and alteration orders for garment and textile shops. Invite-only pilot first."
- **Do not say:** barcode billing, fabric by metre, correct GST for every garment, festival sales promises.
- **Ask:** "Shall I add your shop to the pilot list?"

## Wash (car wash and detailing)
- **Worry:** "Cash at the end of the day does not match." / "Monthly pass washes are not counted." / "I do not know which worker did how many cars."
- **Rupee example (Assumption):** Rs 300 short each day is Rs 9,000 a month.
- **Say today (pilot):** "Jobs, monthly passes and cash closing for car wash and detailing centres. Invite-only pilot first."
- **Do not say:** photos, WhatsApp "car ready" messages, more washes per day.
- **Ask:** "May I add your centre to the pilot list?"

## Hire (recruitment agencies)
- **Worry:** "Candidates are in WhatsApp and Excel and I lose track." / "The client asks for an update and I search."
- **Rupee example (Assumption):** One placement lost a month through a missed follow-up, at an assumed Rs 20,000 fee.
- **Say today (pilot):** "A simple hiring tool for small recruitment agencies. Invite-only pilot first."
- **Do not say:** anything about messaging candidates (lawyer's approval pending), data safety claims, AI matching.
- **Ask:** "Would you like to join the pilot list?"
