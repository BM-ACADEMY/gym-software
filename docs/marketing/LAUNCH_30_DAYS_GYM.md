# 30-day launch calendar: GymDesk invite-only start

Written 13 Nov 2026, not yet tested. Day 1 is the first day a human has confirmed the gym app works on a real database (QA workbook gate). Until then do only the "prepare" items. Numbers (targets) are **Assumptions** to adjust: they are not forecasts.

| Days | Focus | Actions | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before day 1 | Prepare | Dev A and Staff pass the gym screens. Waiting-list screen checked in Super Admin. Gym page `marketing/site/gym/` published with the real API address. Legal pages live (terms, privacy, refund, grievance). Native review of Tamil lines. | Kamar, Dev A |
| 1-3 | Warm list | Write down 30 gym owners you already know (name, town). Message each one personally using Script 2 (`SCRIPTS.md`). Add replies to the waiting list. | Kamar |
| 4-7 | First 5 trials | Invite the first 5 who answered yes. Walk each through setup on a call. Record every problem. | Kamar, Staff |
| 8-10 | Fix week | Fix what the 5 hit. Update the claims file only for things a human saw work. | Claude, Dev A |
| 11-14 | Next 10 trials | Invite 10 more from the list. Ask each for one worry in their own words. | Staff |
| 15 | Check | Fill `WEEKLY_NUMBERS.tsv`. Decide: continue, slow down or fix. | Kamar |
| 16-20 | Local content | 3 Instagram posts using true-now claims (trial, no card, prepaid, GST invoice, import). No numbers. | DM team |
| 21-24 | Referrals | Ask happy trial owners if they know another gym owner. Do not offer rewards yet (no approved offer). | Staff |
| 25-27 | Case notes | With written permission only, write a case note (`TESTIMONIAL_TEMPLATES.md`). | Staff |
| 28-30 | Review | Trials that ended: who paid, who did not, why. Update objections list. Plan month 2. | Kamar |

## Guardrails
- Invite-only: no paid ads in the first 30 days.
- A trial owner is never contacted again after saying stop.
- Do not say the gym app is "live for everyone". Say "invite-only start".
