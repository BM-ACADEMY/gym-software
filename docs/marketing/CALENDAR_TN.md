# Tamil Nadu festival and season calendar

Written 13 Nov 2026. **Every date is an Assumption: check the current year's panchangam or calendar before using.** Many festivals move each year. Use the season to choose the worry to ask about, not to promise results.

| Season or festival | Usual time (Assumption) | Who feels it | Gentle marketing angle (ask, do not promise) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pongal / Thai Pongal and Mattu Pongal | mid January | Cloth, Mart, Dine, Salon | Busy week. Ask: "Is your billing ready for the rush?" |
| New Year resolutions | January | Gym | Gyms get new joiners; many leave by March. Ask about renewals. |
| Summer (hot months) | April to June | Wash, Dine, Salon | Slower or faster hours? Ask what their slow hours are. |
| Exam season (school and college) | March to April | Gym (younger members), Salon | Attendance drops; ask who has stopped coming. |
| Wedding season (muhurtham months) | many dates, often Jan to Feb, and Apr to Jun, Aug to Sep, Nov to Dec | Cloth, Salon, Dine | Big orders and alteration jobs. Ask: "Do you lose track of alteration or booking dates?" |
| Aadi month | mid July to mid August | Cloth, Mart, Salon | Usually quieter for new purchases; Aadi offers are common. Ask about slow stock. |
| Aadi Perukku | around early August | Mart, Dine | Local gatherings. |
| Vinayagar Chathurthi | August to September | Mart | Stock for puja items. |
| Navaratri and Ayudha Puja | September to October | Wash (vehicle puja), Mart | Vehicle washing before puja; Wash centres get busy. |
| Deepavali (Diwali) | October or November | Cloth, Mart, Dine, Salon | The biggest buying season in Tamil Nadu. Start conversations 4 to 6 weeks before. |
| Karthigai Deepam | November to December | Mart, Dine | Lamps and sweets. |
| Margazhi month | mid December to mid January | Dine, Gym | Early mornings; some businesses slower. |
| School reopening | June | Cloth (uniforms), Mart | Uniform and stationery stock. |

## Use
- Plan DM bursts about 4 weeks before a season, one product at a time.
- Do not run festival offers or discounts in our name: we do not promise any (no pricing is approved).
- Keep a "do not disturb" rule on the festival day itself.
