# What we may say in marketing

Rule: say only what the code does today. Update this list when a feature ships or is removed. Last checked 29 Oct 2026 (written, not yet tested by a person).

## AI: allowed wording
- "AI workout and diet plans for each member" (Growth and Pro, within a monthly limit: proposed 30 on Growth and 100 on Pro, not yet approved by Mohamed, so do not print the numbers). A trainer or owner reviews before sharing. Plans are a starting point, not medical advice.
- "AI weekly summary of your gym with 3 things to act on" (all plans, within the monthly AI allowance).
- "Ask GymDesk: an AI assistant that answers how-to questions from our help articles" (all plans, within the allowance).
- "Your members' names and phone numbers are never sent to the AI for the weekly summary or the assistant." (True for those two. AI plans do send the member's goal, equipment and health notes.)

## AI: do NOT say
- "AI-powered gym" as a blanket statement for everything. Churn risk and the dashboard numbers are normal calculations.
- "AI predicts who will quit" (it is a simple score, shown as risk, not a prediction model).
- "AI chatbot on WhatsApp for your members" (not built).
- "AI replies to your members" or "AI writes your WhatsApp messages" (messages use fixed approved templates).
- "Unlimited AI" or "unlimited AI plans" (each plan has a monthly allowance for questions and a monthly limit on plan generations).

## Other claims that are true now
- 14-day free trial, no card. Plans are prepaid, no auto-renewal.
- GST invoice for every payment (CGST+SGST for Puducherry, IGST elsewhere).
- Import members from Excel or CSV. Import fingerprint/face machine logs from a file.
- WhatsApp reminders for renewals, dues, absence and an owner summary (not on Solo).
- Menus in English, Tamil and Hindi (some screens still English).

## Do NOT say yet

- "Renewal Copilot" (daily list of who to contact, with a ready message in English, Tamil and Hindi): written 28 Oct, not yet tested by a person. After Dev A and the staff pass it, we may say: "Every morning, see who is about to leave and send a ready message from your own WhatsApp in one tap." Do not say it sends messages by itself (automatic sends need Meta approval) and do not say the AI picks who to contact (the ranking is rules; AI only rewrites the wording).
- "Ask GymDesk about your own numbers" (dues, who is expiring, who is absent, collections): written 28 Oct, not yet tested. After tests pass we may say: "Ask a question about your gym in plain words and get the answer from your own records." Do not say the AI reads your member data (our code runs fixed reports; the AI only picks which report) and do not say it can answer anything beyond the listed reports.
- Live sync with biometric machines, door control, mobile apps on the app stores, class booking, payroll, multi-branch, member AutoPay, "No 1 in India", customer counts, testimonials.
