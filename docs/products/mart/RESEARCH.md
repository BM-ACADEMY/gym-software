## What this means for Mart (Assumption unless marked)
1. **We cannot win on billing price.** The market already has a free plan with unlimited invoices (Swipe) and paid billing from about Rs 800 to Rs 5,400 a year. Our draft Mart ladder Rs 149 / 299 / 499 a month is Rs 1,788 / 3,588 / 5,988 a year. That is at or above most billing-only competitors. Marketing "cheaper than competitors" would be false for Mart.
2. **The scope file's own reading holds:** billing is a solved problem. Mart has to sell what billing apps barely cover: expiry clearance, reorder suggestions, udhaar (credit) recovery with WhatsApp reminders, supplier price creep and one monthly "money leaks" summary. I did not find these on the pages read, but that only means the pages do not list them (not that no competitor has them). MRT-R1 stays open until each competitor's own feature page is read.
3. **Offline billing is not a differentiator.** Swipe, Vyapar and Commmerce POS are all described as working offline. Our offline billing is table stakes and must be tested on a real phone before we say it.
4. **Barcode scanning and WhatsApp bill sharing are table stakes** (Swipe, myBillBook). Already in our phase 1 list.
5. **Pricing consequence for Tier 3 (Mohamed decides, no deadline blocking the build):** the Mart ladder probably needs a free or very cheap entry plan (billing and stock only) and charges for the AI and WhatsApp part. Proposal for MRT-P1: a free tier without AI and WhatsApp (cost to us: hosting only), then paid plans where the AI and WhatsApp cost sits. I will put numbers in the floor-price table. This is a proposal, not approved.
