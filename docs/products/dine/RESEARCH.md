## What this means for Dine (Assumption unless marked)
1. **Our price comparison must stay honest.** The pages give three different pictures of Petpooja (no price; Rs 12,000 to 40,000 a plan; Rs 7,000 to 10,000 a year). The scope line "Petpooja renewal about Rs 583 a month" rests on one older page. Landing copy must say "compare quotes" and never state a Petpooja price until a person has a quote in writing (a field agent can ask a restaurant for the bill).
2. **Table stakes at the incumbent:** cloud and offline billing, KOT, unlimited terminals and users, inventory, CRM, 80+ reports, food aggregator integrations, QR ordering, captain app. Our phase 1 list already covers billing, KOT, tables, menu, QR menu and stock. **Aggregator integration is a gap** (we keep it out of phase 1 because it depends on partner access); restaurants that live on Zomato and Swiggy may refuse a product without it. Treat it as the top Phase 2 risk.
3. **Unlimited users and terminals** is standard at Petpooja; per-user pricing would be a disadvantage. Our "unlimited staff logins at one price" rule must apply to Dine.
4. **WhatsApp credits are sold as a bundle in the Marketing plans** (4,000 to 10,000). Our WhatsApp cost must be inside the plan or prepaid, as already decided (D-12 and the shop ladder rule).
5. **Pub features (peg-level stock, tabs, split bills)** are not on any page I read for Petpooja; the pub guide in the scope file says peg-level stock exists but is expensive or hardware-based. Keep the pub mode as a Phase 2 item behind a state-by-state excise check (CA/lawyer).
6. **Still open:** a real quote from 3 restaurants and 1 pub; whether a small cafe really needs KOT and aggregator sync; GST rules for restaurants (5 percent without input credit, 18 percent in some cases, composition) confirmed with the CA (Tier 3).
