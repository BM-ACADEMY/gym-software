## 3. Landing page shape (same for every product)
1. Headline = the tagline; one sentence under it saying who it is for.
2. "What it does for you each day" (3 items, from the safe column only).
3. "Autopilot" block: what it watches, what it suggests, what you control (off / suggest / auto) and the guardrails. Say plainly that it starts in suggest mode.
4. Price (only after Mohamed approves; whole rupees, GST shown separately).
5. Free trial (14 days) and how to start; Tamil and Hindi switch when reviewed by a native reader.
6. Support: phone 10 am to 6 pm IST Monday to Saturday; AI help assistant 24 hours for how-to questions only.
7. Footer: company name, address (78, Lenin Street, Kosapalayam, Puducherry - 605013), terms, privacy, refund (money back in 7 to 14 working days after approval), contact.
