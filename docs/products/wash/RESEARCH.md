## What this suggests (Assumption, needs owner interviews)

1. Indian tools found are garage and workshop tools (job cards, parts, service reminders), not tools for a drive-in car wash with attendants and a monthly pass. A wash-first, rupee-priced, WhatsApp-led tool may be a real gap, but the search was small and listing sites are not the market. Treat as weak evidence.
2. The nearest Indian price points are Rs 399 to Rs 799 (billing period unknown). Our draft Rs 149 / 299 / 499 sits below them, which is a price comparison, not proof of demand.
3. Job cards, service reminders and a customer database are normal in the garage tools, so they cannot be our selling point. The scope file's differences are the vehicle record by plate, the monthly pass with balance, before and after photos, attendant commission and the cash-closing leakage check.
