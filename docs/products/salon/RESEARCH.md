## Problems small Indian salons have (Innoviti blog, a payments company, so it sells payments)
- Cash leaves no digital trail; daily reconciliation across staff is slow; staff accountability and theft risk. Source (page's claim: reconciliation from 2 to 3 hours to 5 to 10 minutes with digital payments; no source given).
- Advance bookings fall through because paying upfront is awkward; page says payment links can improve booking reliability by 30 to 40 percent (no source given). Source, treat as unproven.
- Page estimates cash-dependent salons lose 15 to 20 percent of potential sales (no source given). Do not use this number in our marketing.

## What this means for our Salon scope (Assumption unless marked)
1. **Our phase 1 list matches the market.** Every feature these three vendors list (booking, walk-in queue, GST billing, split pay, WhatsApp reminders, packages and memberships, loyalty, commission, stock, win-back) is already in our phase 1 list. Nothing in this pass says we are missing a must-have.
2. **AI receptionist on WhatsApp** (SalonBoost, AI Growth plan) is a competitor feature we deliberately leave for Phase 2 (needs live WhatsApp approval, and the Afra rule that no autonomous AI messages are sent). Do not claim it.
3. **Google review automation** (SalonBoost) is cheap to add after launch (a review link in the post-visit message, owner sends from WhatsApp). Added to the Phase 2 list as SAL-P2-REV.
4. **Price.** The one India price I found is Rs 799 a month (annual billing) for the cheapest plan. Our draft Rs 149 / 299 / 499 is lower, which fits the "70 to 88 percent cut" claim in the scope file only against the old Rs 800 to 2,500 range. The floor-price table (SAL-P1) still decides what is safe. Not approved.
5. **Offline billing** is not mentioned on the Invoay or SalonBoost pages I read. That is only "the page does not mention it", not "they do not have it". Our claims file must not say competitors lack it; we may say our billing keeps working when the network drops, once a person has tested it on a real phone.
6. **Per-staff login pricing** (Rs 200 to 500 per extra login, from the earlier source) is a real irritation point for salons with many staff; our "unlimited staff logins at one price" stays a headline, still unverified against current vendor pricing.
