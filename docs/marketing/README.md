Public pilot pages are in `marketing/site/<product>/index.html` and post to the waiting list in Super Admin.

## Rules that hold for everything here
1. Say only what a person has seen work. Today that is the pilot invitation for six products and the "true now" list for the gym.
2. No prices in copy until Mohamed approves them. No named-competitor comparisons. No customer counts, ratings or testimonials.
3. Messages go only to people who agreed. Stop on "stop". No AI voice calls. No automatic sending of WhatsApp marketing.
4. Real leads and phone numbers never go in git, docs or chat. They live in the Super Admin waiting list.

## Weekly numbers sheet: what each column means
`week_start` Monday date; `product` fit, groom, mart, dine, cloth, wash or hire; `leads` new waiting-list entries that week (from Super Admin); `demos` calls or walk-throughs held; `trials` trials started; `paid` first payments received; `churned` paid accounts that ended; `notes` one line. Count people, never store phone numbers here. Dates in the first rows are Assumptions; adjust.
